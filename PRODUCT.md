# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Users
Teens and adults (13+) who want a calm, short session, often in the evening. They play one or two nights of 1–2 minutes, one-thumbed, on a phone. They come back for the Daily Tide and to finish a season.

## Product Purpose
Keeper of the Cove is an offline mobile game. You keep a lighthouse and sweep its beam to guide lost vessels home through the dark. Success: the player feels gently responsible for the boats, finishes a season, and returns the next day. Nothing is sold and no data leaves the device.

## Positioning
The only tool is one beam, and attention is the scarce resource. Tension comes from choosing which vessel to light when two need you at once, not from combat or harsh failure. You guide; you never harm.

## Operating Context
- Played portrait, one thumb; the lower part of the screen is the thumb's sweep zone.
- Sessions are short: one night lasts 60–100 s; a season takes 10–25 minutes.
- Released on Google Play by a client publisher. Publisher details appear only in `docs/PRIVACY_POLICY.md` and the Play listing, never in the app. Signed builds come only from GitHub Actions.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- 52 nights across 4 seasons (Still Water, Fog Season, Squall Coast, The Long Winter), Daily Tide, The Longest Night (endless), 20 achievements, Keeper's Log stats, Chandlery cosmetic shop paid with earned lamp oil.
- Fully offline. No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no analytics, no permissions in the release manifest.
- Flutter, Android only. Art and sound are generated by scripts in `tool/`; no third-party media.
- Terminology: night (level), season (chapter), vessel / soul, embers (lives), lanterns (1–3 rating), lamp oil (earned currency), dawn (night timer), the harbor.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: **Keeper of the Cove**. In-world copy voice: quiet, warm, a keeper's logbook ("Tend the Light", "Relight the watch", "Sail on", "Light it again").
- Light is safety, dark is danger. Nothing sharp, nothing loud. Lost vessels fade into the dark; nothing wrecks or dies on screen.
- Binding visual constraints confirmed by the user: keep the amber beam on an indigo sea, the Spectral serif, and navigation by tapping things in the scene.

## Evidence on Hand
- Spectral font (OFL) at `assets/fonts/`. Existing icon source `assets/icon/app_icon.png` (will be replaced by a scripted logo).
- No reviews, players, press or download numbers exist. Never invent them.

## Product Principles
1. Gentle guardianship: every mechanic is about guiding, never harming.
2. One beam, limited attention: new seasons add choices, not new controls.
3. Calm, not empty: the sea feels alive; failure is soft and retry is instant.
4. Earned, never sold: everything unlockable is paid for by playing.
5. Private by default: nothing leaves the device.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Never rely on colour alone (charting ring shape, lantern counts, ember icons with count).
- Touch targets ≥ 48 dp; Semantics labels on scene hotspots.
- Reduce-motion setting that also respects the system setting; sound, music and vibration toggles.
